DeAddict Support

For account, sync, purchase, privacy, accessibility, or technical help, contact MiCirql at support@micirql.com.

Email support

Include in your request

  • A short description of the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • Your DeAddict app version and Android version.
  • The approximate date and time of the problem.
  • Screenshots with sensitive recovery information hidden.
  • For purchase issues, the Google Play order identifier. Never send payment-card details or passwords.

Common help

Sign-in or sync issue

Confirm that the device is online, update DeAddict, restart the app, and retry. Do not repeatedly create replacement accounts if your existing account contains recovery data.

Restore a purchase

Use “Restore purchases” in DeAddict while signed into the Google Play account used for the purchase. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Google Play.

Delete an account

Use the in-app deletion option or follow the external process on the Delete Account page.

Safety

Support email is not an emergency or clinical service. If you may be in immediate danger, are considering self-harm, or have severe withdrawal symptoms, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional immediately.

Privacy requests

Use the subject “Privacy Request” and describe whether you are requesting access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion. MiCirql may ask for reasonable verification to protect the account.
