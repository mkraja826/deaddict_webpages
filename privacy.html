Privacy Policy

Effective: July 28, 2026 · Developer: MiCirql · App package: com.deaddict.app

This policy is a production-readiness draft and must remain consistent with the app, backend configuration, SDKs, and Google Play Data safety declarations.

1. Overview

MiCirql operates DeAddict, a recovery and habit-management application. This policy explains how DeAddict processes information when you create an account, use recovery features, synchronize data, purchase a subscription, contact support, export data, or request deletion.

2. Information processed

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers, and sign-in provider information.
  • Recovery information: selected addiction or habit programs, goals, check-ins, streaks, notes, urge or focus-session records, and preferences you choose to save.
  • Technical information: app version, device and operating-system details, diagnostic events, synchronization status, and security-related records needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Purchase information: product, entitlement, and purchase-verification status. Payment-card details are processed by Google Play and are not collected directly by MiCirql.
  • Support communications: messages and attachments you send to support.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, provide recovery features, synchronize authorized data, restore purchases, prevent abuse, secure accounts, troubleshoot errors, respond to support requests, comply with legal obligations, and improve reliability. DeAddict does not use recovery information to provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. Storage and service providers

DeAddict uses local device storage and may use Supabase for authentication, database, synchronization, and server functions. Google services may be used for sign-in, app distribution, purchase processing, crash diagnostics, or platform functionality where configured. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

5. Sharing

MiCirql does not sell personal or sensitive information. Information may be shared with contracted service providers strictly to operate DeAddict, with authorities when legally required, or during a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. Recovery information is not shared for third-party advertising.

6. Security

MiCirql uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including access controls and encryption in transit where supported. No system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Retention

Account and recovery data is retained while your account is active and for only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here. After a verified deletion request, associated account data is deleted or anonymized unless limited records must be retained for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or other legal obligations. Backup deletion may require a limited operational period.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal information. You can use in-app controls, visit the account deletion page, or email support@micirql.com. Identity verification may be required to protect your account.

9. Children

DeAddict is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. MiCirql does not knowingly collect personal information from children who cannot legally consent. Contact support if you believe a child has provided information improperly.

10. International use

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, MiCirql will use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when features, providers, laws, or practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the app, website, or other appropriate channel, and the effective date will be revised.
