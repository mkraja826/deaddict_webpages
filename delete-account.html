Delete your DeAddict account

This page lets DeAddict users request deletion without reinstalling the app. DeAddict is developed by MiCirql and uses package name com.deaddict.app.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open DeAddict and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Open Privacy and security.
  4. Select Delete account and complete the confirmation steps.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address associated with your DeAddict account. Use the subject “Delete my DeAddict account” and state that you want the account and associated data permanently deleted.

Request account deletion

Email: support@micirql.com

Verification

MiCirql may ask you to verify control of the account or associated email before completing deletion. Do not send passwords, recovery notes, payment-card details, or government identity documents unless MiCirql provides a secure and legally necessary verification method.

What is deleted

After verification, MiCirql will delete or irreversibly anonymize the DeAddict account and associated recovery information stored in active systems, including goals, check-ins, streaks, focus or urge-session records, preferences, and synchronized app data associated with that account.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, transaction verification, or other legal obligations. Data in protected backups may remain for a limited backup lifecycle before automatic deletion. Retained data will not be used to continue providing an active DeAddict account.

Subscriptions

Deleting your DeAddict account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription separately through Google Play to prevent future renewal charges. Account deletion can proceed independently of subscription cancellation.

Processing

MiCirql will acknowledge deletion requests and complete verified requests within the period required by applicable law. You will be informed if additional verification or a lawful retention exception applies.

Need help?

Contact support@micirql.com or visit the support page.
