Medical Disclaimer

Effective: July 28, 2026

DeAddict is not emergency, medical, psychiatric, or addiction-treatment care.

General information only

DeAddict provides self-management tools, educational information, habit tracking, recovery goals, reminders, focus sessions, and links to safety resources. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, addiction, withdrawal condition, or mental-health condition.

No clinical relationship

Using DeAddict does not create a doctor-patient, therapist-client, counselor-client, or other healthcare-professional relationship with MiCirql. App content and automated feedback are not individualized medical advice.

Withdrawal and detoxification

Do not rely on DeAddict for detoxification, tapering, medication changes, or withdrawal management. Withdrawal from alcohol, sedatives, opioids, and other substances can be dangerous or life-threatening. Seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional before making changes that may cause withdrawal.

Emergency situations

If you may be in immediate danger, are considering self-harm or harming someone else, have taken an overdose, or have severe symptoms such as seizures, loss of consciousness, breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, hallucinations, or uncontrolled vomiting, contact local emergency services immediately.

Professional care

Always seek advice from an appropriately qualified physician, mental-health professional, addiction specialist, or other licensed professional regarding medical symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, medication, or recovery planning. Never delay professional care because of information shown in DeAddict.

Personal responsibility

You are responsible for decisions made using the app. Recovery outcomes vary, and streaks, reminders, insights, or other app features do not guarantee safety or recovery.
